# Vendor Onboarding — Standard Operating Procedure (v3, last edited 2025-11-02)

Owner: Procurement (Jin Park)

1. **Submit a vendor request** in Jira (requester). Include the vendor name, the spend estimate and the business reason.
2. **Procurement review** (Procurement, within 2 business days). Check the spend estimate and look for an existing vendor.
3. **Security questionnaire** (Security). Send the questionnaire to the vendor by email.
4. **Security review** (Security, in Vanta). **Required for every vendor. No exceptions.**
5. **Contract review** (Legal, in Ironclad). This starts only after security approves.
6. **Create the vendor in NetSuite** (Finance).
7. **Notify the requester** (Procurement).

Target: 10 business days end to end.
